## About

I am currently an **Agent Engineer at ByteDance**, working on practical systems that connect language models with tools, memory, retrieval, and evaluation. My current interests sit at the boundary between model capability and dependable software: orchestration, context engineering, long-running workflows, and observability.

I hold both my bachelor's and master's degrees from **Shanghai Jiao Tong University**. Earlier in my career, I worked as a software engineer on autonomous-driving systems at **WeRide**. That experience shaped how I think about reliability, interfaces, and failure modes; today, my main focus is agent engineering.

> **When things are ambiguous, take one deliberate step forward.**

## What I am exploring

- **Agent architecture** — tool use, planning, multi-agent coordination, and durable workflows
- **Knowledge systems** — hybrid retrieval, MCP servers, memory, and context compression
- **Evaluation** — trace-based analysis, task success, regressions, and human-in-the-loop review
- **Production engineering** — observability, failure handling, latency, and maintainable interfaces
